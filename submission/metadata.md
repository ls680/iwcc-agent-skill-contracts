## Short contribution statement

IWCC compiles successful agent traces into deterministic, provenance-carrying
structural contracts and uses them to screen complete skill-program revisions
before environment execution. A frozen, zero-overlap native confirmation on 60
ALFWorld and ScienceWorld tasks shows a 10.64-point balanced-accuracy gain over
the predeclared strongest comparator, with a task-stratified 95% interval of
8.88--12.50 points and no observed rejection among 98 controls.
