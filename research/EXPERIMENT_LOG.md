## 2026-09-06: development mechanism V2 passed

- V2 retains concrete entity instances for state fluents, separately
  normalizes only objective roles, learns ScienceWorld room transitions from
  successful traces, and records native-surviving revisions as alternative
  successful branch witnesses.
- On the same exposed development corpus, V2 achieved 87.21% balanced
  accuracy, 75.73% recall, and 98.70% specificity. It improved 12.62 points
  over endpoint-only and ordered-milestone contracts, with positive effects in
  both ALFWorld and ScienceWorld.
- One hundred shuffled trace insertion orders yielded the same canonical
  contract hash; every emitted clause retained at least one witness.
- Three local model judges completed 540 program reviews. Qwen rejected all
  programs; Phi-4-mini and Mistral-7B also produced high false-rejection rates.
- An initial nearest-trace calculation was discarded because it included the
  candidate task's own healthy program. Leave-one-task-out recalculation at the
  development-calibrated 0.6 threshold yielded 51.61% balanced accuracy.
  Endpoint-only is therefore the strongest valid development comparator at
  74.59%, and V2 passes all strong-control development gates.
- Confirmation remains unopened pending complete method/roster/analyzer
  freeze.
